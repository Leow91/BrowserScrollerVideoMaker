# Social Media Platform Guide

Complete guide for creating optimized videos for different social media platforms.

## 📱 Quick Platform Selector

The Web GUI now includes a **Platform Dropdown** that automatically configures video settings for your target platform!

### How to Use

1. Open the Web GUI (`python web_gui.py`)
2. Go to **"📹 Single Video"** tab
3. Select your platform from the **"🎯 Social Media Platform"** dropdown
4. Settings are automatically adjusted!
5. Generate your video

## 📊 Supported Platforms

### Vertical Formats (9:16 - Mobile-First)

#### 📱 Instagram Story
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 (9:16)
- **Duration:** 3-15 seconds (max 15s)
- **Recommended:** 10 seconds
- **Use Case:** Quick, attention-grabbing content
- **Tips:** Keep important content in the center safe zone

#### 🎬 Instagram Reel
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 (9:16)
- **Duration:** 3-90 seconds (max 90s)
- **Recommended:** 30 seconds
- **Use Case:** Engaging short-form content
- **Tips:** First 3 seconds are crucial for engagement

#### 📱 Facebook Story
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 (9:16)
- **Duration:** 3-20 seconds (max 20s)
- **Recommended:** 10 seconds
- **Use Case:** Behind-the-scenes, announcements
- **Tips:** Similar to Instagram Stories

#### 💬 WhatsApp Status
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 (9:16)
- **Duration:** 3-30 seconds (max 30s)
- **Recommended:** 15 seconds
- **Use Case:** Personal updates, quick messages
- **Tips:** Keep text large and readable

#### 🎵 TikTok
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 (9:16)
- **Duration:** 3-180 seconds (max 3 minutes)
- **Recommended:** 30 seconds
- **Use Case:** Viral content, trends, entertainment
- **Tips:** Hook viewers in first second, use trending sounds

#### ▶️ YouTube Shorts
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 (9:16)
- **Duration:** 3-60 seconds (max 60s)
- **Recommended:** 30 seconds
- **Use Case:** Quick tips, previews, highlights

#### 📌 Pinterest Pin
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 (9:16)
- **Duration:** 4-60 seconds (max 60s)
- **Recommended:** 15 seconds

#### 👻 Snapchat

#### 💼 LinkedIn

## 🎯 Best Practices by Format

### Vertical (9:16)
- ✅ Mobile-first viewing
- ✅ Full-screen immersive
- ✅ Ideal for Stories, Reels, TikTok
- ⚠️ Keep text in safe zones (avoid top/bottom 250px)
- ⚠️ Design for portrait orientation

### Square (1:1)
- ✅ Universal compatibility
- ✅ Works on mobile and desktop
- ✅ Clean grid appearance
- ✅ Good for products and graphics
- ⚠️ Less screen real estate

### Landscape (16:9)
- ✅ Traditional format
- ✅ Desktop-optimized
- ✅ Cinematic feel
- ✅ More horizontal space
- ⚠️ Not ideal for mobile Stories

## 🎨 Design Recommendations

### Text Overlays
- **Size:** 48-72pt for mobile
- **Color:** White with black outline for readability
- **Position:** Center or bottom third
- **Duration:** Min 3 seconds on screen

### Logos
- **Size:** 200-300px width for 1920px videos
- **Position:** Top corner (usually top-right)
- **Opacity:** 80-100%
- **Format:** PNG with transparency

### Safe Zones
- **Vertical:** Keep content 250px from top/bottom
- **Horizontal:** Keep content 10% from edges
- **Square:** Center-focused design
- **Reason:** UI elements, profile pics, buttons

- **Videos:** Thumbnail matters (first frame)

- **Length:** 30-60s ideal

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Design for Sound-Off**
   - 85% of social media is watched without sound
   - Always add captions or text overlays

2. **Hook in 3 Seconds**
   - Most users decide to keep watching in first 3s
   - Make opening frame compelling

3. **Test on Mobile**
   - Majority of views are on mobile
   - Check readability on small screens

4. **Platform-Specific Optimization**
   - Don't just repurpose
   - Optimize each version for its platform

5. **File Size Matters**
   - Keep under 50MB for Instagram
   - Compress for WhatsApp (under 16MB)

6. **Safe Zones Are Critical**
   - Profile pics, buttons, UI overlap content
   - Keep important elements in safe zones

7. **Consistent Branding**
   - Use same logo position across platforms
   - Maintain brand colors and fonts
